  • Indoor Tracking & Positioning

  • Location Analytics & Heatmaps
  • Hoopoe Ad Manager

Restaurants, cafés, food courts and drive-thrus. Turn the Wi-Fi a guest already needs into an interactive ordering, loyalty, and entertainment layer.

  • Wi-Fi Intelligent Menu

  • Loyalty & cross-app profiles
  • Hoopoe Ad Manager

Conferences, expos, summits and pop-ups. Spin up branded Wi-Fi, capture lead data, and run sponsor campaigns in one console.

About Hoopoe Digital

Pioneers in Spatial Intelligence.

A Gen-AI field focused on transforming physical venues into data-driven, intelligent spaces that interact with visitors, offer services, and respond in real time.

Our story

Wi-Fi was the missing foundation for spatial intelligence.

Wi-Fi is present in virtually every venue but vastly underutilized beyond basic connectivity. Hoopoe Digital unlocks its hidden potential by transforming passive network signals into actionable business intelligence — capturing rich behavioral and demographic insights from every visitor, whether they connect or not.

Using Smart Wi-Fi alongside Digital Twin and other AI technologies, we enable seamless interaction, enhanced experiences, and intelligent operations within physical spaces.

What makes an Intelligent Venue

  • Interacts with visitors in real time
  • Offers services without an app download
  • Answers questions and predicts needs
  • Captures anonymous behavioral data
  • Learns and adapts over time
